## What Submission 1 contains

This submission requests examination of barriers facing independent agricultural equipment and perception technology, including grower evaluation, integration, financing, distribution, licensing, support and litigation-related effects. It contains a source-qualified chronology and selected supporting records. Its key question is whether farmers have a meaningful opportunity to evaluate competing technologies on technical merit and whole-season cost.

**Chronology counting:** The October 29 PDF includes **158 event entries** (73 entries already disclosed in the public chronology and 85 additional entries). The public chronology identifies 166 entries; eight were set aside for separate legal or privilege review and are **not** included in the October 29 agency package. Thus “unredacted” does not mean that every underlying document, reserved entry or separate native file has been included.
